Our story

Two legends.
One roof.

Dozens × Fatboys isn't a new concept — it's a preservation act. A tribute to two Aurora institutions that shaped how a neighborhood eats, drinks, and gathers.

1937

A Legend Is Born

Fatboy is born in Chicago — reportedly 26 pounds at birth. His philosophy will one day become a restaurant creed.

1988

Fatboys Opens

Janet Walker opens Fatboys Sports Bar & Grill in Aurora, Colorado. It becomes a 35-year neighborhood staple.

2000s

The Dozens Rises

Dozens Restaurant becomes Aurora's beloved breakfast spot — silver-dollar pancakes and coffee cups that never empty.

Recently

A Legacy Preserved

When Dozens closes, longtime customer Don Hicks buys the concept to keep the tradition alive.

Today

One Roof

Don partners with restaurateur Wesley Wacker to merge Dozens into Fatboys. Two Aurora legends. One unforgettable spot.
